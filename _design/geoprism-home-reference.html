Curate, govern and share geographic knowledge through time.

Geoprism Registry helps organizations keep a shared, authoritative record of geographic places, their boundaries, relationships and history, so that data from many systems lines up, and stays correct as the map changes.

Why it matters

Location and time are what bind information together. When they disagree, everything built on them does too.

01

Agencies use different lists of places

A health ministry, a statistics office and a relief program each keep their own district names and codes. Their data can't be combined without manual matching.

02

Boundaries change, and history breaks

When a district splits or merges, years of trend data are quietly compared against the wrong geography unless the change itself is recorded.

03

AI doesn't know your geography

Language models have no reliable knowledge of which places exist, how they relate, or how they've changed. They need a trusted, traceable source to draw on.

How it works

From scattered datasets to shared, trusted knowledge

Each organization keeps curating the data it is mandated to maintain. Geoprism Registry connects that work through common identifiers, common definitions and a full record of where every value came from.

  1. Step 1

    Integrate

    Bring in place lists, boundaries and related records from each responsible authority, keeping the source of every value.

  2. Step 2

    Enhance

    Link places into hierarchies and networks, classify them with shared vocabularies, and track every change through time.

  3. Step 3

    Publish

    Share versioned, authoritative geographies and knowledge graphs with information systems, analysts and AI tools.

  4. Step 4

    Assess

    [CONFIRM COPY] Explore and evaluate the published knowledge, trace any answer back to its source, and act on what it shows.

Where it's used

A common geography for work that crosses sectors

Public health

Align facility and district data across health information systems for microplanning, campaigns and trend analysis, including FHIR export.

National statistics

Publish common geographies for census and survey results so statistics can be compared across agencies and across years.

Spatial data infrastructure

Host and regularly update the core geographic objects a country relies on, while respecting each agency's curation mandate.

AI and knowledge graphs

Give AI systems trusted, time-aware geographic knowledge, while organizations keep sovereignty over their data.

See all features

Publish knowledge graphs

Share versioned spatial knowledge graphs of places, programs and concepts, synchronized to standard graph databases.

Shared vocabularies

Classify places with controlled terms and taxonomies everyone agrees on.

Link programs and people to places

Manage non-spatial records like programs, staff or survey results alongside the geography they relate to.

Beyond hierarchies

Model real-world networks, like rivers that flow into each other or areas that share a border.

Know where every value came from

Record which authority is responsible for each dataset and which source supplied each value.

Undo any import

Every import creates a checkpoint you can roll back, so mistakes never become permanent.
